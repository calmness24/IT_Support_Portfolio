# Printer Troubleshooting Guide

## Scenario

A user reports that they are unable to print documents from their computer.

## Objective

Identify and resolve common printer issues to restore printing functionality.

## Troubleshooting Steps

1. Confirm that the printer is powered on.
2. Verify that the printer is connected to the computer or network.
3. Check for any paper jams or error messages on the printer.
4. Ensure the correct printer is selected as the default printer.
5. Restart the printer and computer.
6. Print a test page to verify functionality.
