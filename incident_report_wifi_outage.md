# Incident Report: Wi-Fi Connectivity Outage

## Incident Summary 

A user reported that they were unable to connect to the company Wi-Fi network and could not access internet resources required for their work. 

## Priority

Medium

## Investigation

- Confirmed that the user's device could not connect to the Wi-Fi network.
- Verified that other users were able to connect successfully.
- Checked the device Wi-Fi settings and confirmed the correct network was selected.
- Restarted the Wi-Fi adapter and reconnected to the network.
- Tested internet connectivity after reconnection.
